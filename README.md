One funny new name for a planet in our solar system, plus the why, in a single line — for make.fun's "Rename the Planet" bounty, $3.10 in prizes. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with no network and no randomness.

> Venus is now 'The Ex': hottest thing in the solar system, spins backwards, and rains acid on anyone who tries to come back.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). The line is 123 characters — inside the 1–200 gate, UTF-8, one line.

## Why this line

The joke is an idea, not a pun on the planet's name. Venus is the hottest planet in the solar system, it spins backwards (retrograde rotation), and its clouds rain sulfuric acid — three traits no other planet shares at once. An ex is hot, goes backwards, and turns toxic the moment you come back: the why earns the name, and the name would fit no other planet.
